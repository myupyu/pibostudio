This guide introduces the basic flow of Jangdok Garden while it is in development. Screen locations and wording may change before release.

## 1. Check ingredients and choose a batch

Begin with one food and the ingredients it needs. Distinguish what you can prepare now from what needs another ingredient before planning your next action.

## 2. Check the batch after preparing it

Look at fermentation and the weather. Rather than only advancing the calendar, notice what care the jars need today.

## 3. Put ready food on the shelf

Take finished food out, display it, and continue to a sale. Check both what you have made and what is on the shelf when preparing for an order.

## 4. Read orders and letters

Neighbors’ orders name the foods they need. When a conversation or letter arrives, read it and check any new promise. Move between tending the workshop and following its stories.

## 5. Check saving before changing devices

Progress is stored on your device. Before deleting the app or changing devices, use save export in settings to keep a separate copy. Importing a save can replace current progress, so export the current record first.
