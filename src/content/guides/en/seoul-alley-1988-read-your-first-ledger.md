Seoul Alley 1988 is in development. This guide introduces the things to check when you first take over the shop, without promising a particular strategy’s profit.

## Separate sales from the money that remains

Sales record the amount received for goods. That figure is not automatically what remains after buying stock and paying operating costs. Read the business results alongside current cash.

## Look at stock before the next purchase

Check what is still on the shelf. Use the last day’s results to decide whether to buy more of the same goods or prepare something different. Consider prices with that plan.

## Include the household at month-end

Look at shop costs and household expenses together. When considering another space or a different operation, check what will remain for the next day’s business and everyday living after the initial expense.

## Remember promises made in conversation

People’s stories run alongside the shop. Read new conversations and look back through memories and records at earlier choices. This guide keeps later events and endings out of view.

## Keep a copy before moving your progress

Progress is stored on your device. Before changing devices or deleting the app, export a save from settings. Keep an export of current progress before importing another file, too.
