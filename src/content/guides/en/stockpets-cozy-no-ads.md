> **StockPets** is a cozy pet and farm game that begins in 1996. Start for free, care for your companion and turn the calendar when you are ready for the next month.

## A little world for a few quiet minutes

Plant a seed, check your crops and spend a moment with your companion. Instead of focusing only on a return, notice what grew this month and how your little friend has changed.

Historical US stock price changes shape the crops, but no real stocks are traded. The game’s virtual coins cannot be deposited, withdrawn or exchanged for cash.

## The next month starts with you

The first 12 months can be advanced freely. After that, one calendar charges every eight hours, up to three. Advance one month or use three calendars together.

Game time moves when you turn the calendar. Look around your farm, care for your companion and decide when to meet new prices and little life events.

## A farm that grows with a friend

Basic care includes feeding, playing and cleaning. Your companion grows from baby to young pet to adult, with care and harvest experiences helping shape its grown appearance.

Both a good harvest and a decision to wait become part of the journey. Explore [how pets grow](/stockpets/guides/pet-growth/) to discover more about the time you share.

## A story saved on your device

Your journey is stored on your device without a login or cloud sync. Export your record in Settings before resetting or changing your device.

If you want a fresh start, Settings offers Start over. Exporting your previous record first lets you keep a copy of that farm’s memories.

## Begin with one little seed

StockPets is available to start for free on iPhone and Android, in 17 languages on iPhone, including English and Korean.

For a first-month walkthrough, see [how to play](/stockpets/guides/how-to-play/). A familiar company’s seed and a companion you like are a lovely place to begin.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I start for free?

Yes. You can start StockPets for free on iPhone and Android.

### Do I have to play every day?

Your journey advances when you turn the calendar. After the first 12 months, one calendar charges every eight hours, up to three.

### Does progress sync to another device?

Cloud sync is not provided. Export your record in Settings before resetting or changing devices.

StockPets uses virtual coins only. This article is not investment advice, and past price changes do not predict future results.
