## Read the order slip first

Open for service and choose today’s guest. Dialogue and an order follow. Read who is here and what they asked for, then use the scene’s cooking cue and buttons to find the next action.

## Prepare the dish by hand

Direct preparation asks you to work with the scene’s ingredients and tools. Each food uses different gestures. When cooking is finished, choose a finishing touch and serve the prepared food. Dishes with a heat choice let you select its level directly.

## Stay for the response

Read the guest’s words and response after the meal. An imperfect dish does not stop the essential story. Read the service result before preparing the next opening.

## If the gestures feel difficult

Explore relaxed timing and calm controls in Settings. Larger text, reduced motion, music, effects, and haptic options are also available. You do not need to grind for convenience tools first.

Return to [the little shop](/midnightbunsik/) for a few more moments from the game.
