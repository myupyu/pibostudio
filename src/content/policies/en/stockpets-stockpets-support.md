## Get in touch

Contact [support@pibostudio.com](mailto:support@pibostudio.com?subject=stockpets) with the app version, device model, OS version, and steps to reproduce. Attaching a save is optional. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

## Your journey stays on your device

Export your record in the game’s Settings before changing devices or deleting the app. Automatic cloud sync is not provided.

## Purchases and platforms

Ad-removal purchases on iPhone can be restored with the same store account. Check the current store listing and [StockPets official policy](https://stockpets.com/en/privacy/) for platform-specific terms and privacy information.
