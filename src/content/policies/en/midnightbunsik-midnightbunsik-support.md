## Get in touch

Contact [support@pibostudio.com](mailto:support@pibostudio.com?subject=midnightbunsik) with the app version, device model, OS version, and steps to reproduce. Attaching a save is optional. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

## Your journey stays on your device

Export your record in the game’s Settings before changing devices or deleting the app. Automatic cloud sync is not provided.

[Save and return guide](/midnightbunsik/guides/save-and-return/) · [Privacy policy](/midnightbunsik/privacy/)


Ad removal is an optional one-time purchase in Settings. Restore it with the same Apple Account. New games and save imports do not alter ownership. Approved pending purchases update automatically. The story remains playable when ads or the network are unavailable.
