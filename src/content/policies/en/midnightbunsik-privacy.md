## Progress on your device

Progress, settings, in-game currency and decorations, and recent game events stay on this device. They are not sent to a developer server. There are no game accounts or cloud saves. The main story, epilogue, and every dish are free and remain playable offline.

## Banners and privacy choices

The iOS app uses Google AdMob banners. The Android release will use the same approach. Banners are hidden during the first night, dialogue, orders, cooking, meals, and farewells. When ads are requested, Google may process IP-based approximate location, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, and crash, performance, and diagnostic information. We do not add a separate game analytics SDK; the advertising SDK performs advertising measurement and diagnostics. We do not request precise location access.

We request non-personalized ads, disable publisher first-party identifiers and publisher privacy personalization, and do not request iOS tracking authorization. Where required, Google presents a consent message. Where applicable, Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you change or withdraw your choices. The game continues if consent cannot be checked or an ad cannot load. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Optional ad removal

Ad removal is an optional one-time non-consumable purchase. The App Store processes payments on iOS and Google Play processes them on Android. The purchase sheet displays the local price and currency. There are no subscriptions or paid game currency. We do not receive payment-card details. The app verifies the store purchase on the device. We have no purchase server. To restore, use Restore purchases in Settings with the account and store you bought it from. Purchases do not transfer between stores. New games and save imports do not alter ownership. Once ad removal is verified, the app does not request ads. The store that processed the purchase handles refunds.

## Export and support

An export writes the save to the location you choose. On iOS, you can also export to a sharing destination you choose. External sharing and backup services follow their own policies. Support messages and reply addresses are kept only as needed to resolve the request, then deleted unless legal retention applies. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

## Deletion and contact

Start a new game resets active progress. Preserved damaged originals and recovery copies may remain for recovery. Deleting the app may delete device data; export first if you want a backup. Delete exported files and external copies yourself. Store purchase records and ownership follow the policies of the store you used. Advertising data held by Google follows Google’s policies and controls. The app is not specifically directed to children and does not directly request their personal information.
