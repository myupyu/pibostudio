Effective October 15, 2026 · Pibo Studio / GATEPOP Inc.

## Progress on your device

Progress, settings, in-game currency and decorations, and recent game events stay on this device. They are not sent to a developer server. There are no game accounts or cloud saves. The main story, epilogue, and every dish are free and remain playable offline.

## Banners and privacy choices

The iPhone app uses Google AdMob banners. Banners are hidden during the first night, dialogue, orders, cooking, meals, and farewells. When ads are requested, Google may process IP-based approximate location, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, and crash, performance, and diagnostic information. We do not add a separate game analytics SDK; the advertising SDK performs advertising measurement and diagnostics. We do not request precise location access.

We request non-personalized ads, disable publisher first-party identifiers and publisher privacy personalization, and do not request iOS tracking authorization. Where required, Google presents a consent message. Where applicable, Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you change or withdraw your choices. The game continues if consent cannot be checked or an ad cannot load. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Optional ad removal

Ad removal is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed by Apple. The Korean base price is KRW 4,900; confirm your actual price and currency in Apple's purchase sheet. There are no subscriptions or paid game currency. We do not receive payment-card details. Ownership comes from Apple's verified on-device transactions, with no developer purchase server. Restore purchases in Settings using the same Apple Account. New games and save imports do not alter ownership. Once ad removal is verified, the app does not request ads. Apple handles refunds.

## Export and support

An export sends a save only to the Files location or sharing destination you choose. External sharing and backup services follow their own policies. Support messages and reply addresses are kept only as needed to resolve the request, then deleted unless legal retention applies. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

## Deletion and contact

Start a new game resets active progress. Preserved damaged originals and recovery copies may remain for recovery. Deleting the app may delete device data; export first if you want a backup. Delete exported files and external copies yourself. Apple purchases and data held by Google follow their respective policies and choice procedures. The app is not specifically directed to children and does not directly request their personal information.
