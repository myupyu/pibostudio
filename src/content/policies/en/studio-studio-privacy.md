## Information used by this website

The Pibo Studio website has no login, comments, payment form, advertising SDK, or separate visitor analytics SDK. A light or dark theme preference is stored in your browser to remember your display choice.

Loading fonts may connect to Google Fonts and a font-provider CDN. Services delivering the website or its fonts may process IP addresses and basic request information. Their own policies apply to those services.

## Support emails

Messages are sent through the email application you choose. Your message and reply address are used while needed to resolve your request and then deleted, except where legal retention is required. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information.

## Each game has its own privacy information

Progress storage, ads, and purchase conditions differ between products. Please read [Midnight Bunsik’s policy](/midnightbunsik/privacy/) and [StockPets’ official policy](https://stockpets.com/en/privacy/) separately.
