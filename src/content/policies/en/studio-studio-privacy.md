## Information used by this website

The Pibo Studio website has no login, comments, payment form, or advertising SDK. A light or dark theme preference is stored in your browser to remember your display choice.

Loading fonts may connect to Google Fonts and a font-provider CDN. Services delivering the website or its fonts may process IP addresses and basic request information. Their own policies apply to those services.

## Website visitor statistics

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure website page views and visits. Google may process analytics cookies, browser/device and connection information. We do not send form entries, email contents, user IDs, or URL query/fragment values. Only the referring site origin is provided. Pages with query strings and contact/account screens are excluded. Automatic form and site-search measurement, Google signals and ad personalization are disabled. The tag respects the browser’s Do Not Track setting. You can block analytics or delete its cookies in your browser. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). This website measurement does not add analytics to our games.

## Support emails

Messages are sent through the email application you choose. Your message and reply address are used while needed to resolve your request and then deleted, except where legal retention is required. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information.

## Each game has its own privacy information

Progress storage, ads, and purchase conditions differ between products. Please read [Midnight Bunsik’s policy](/midnightbunsik/privacy/) and [StockPets’ official policy](https://stockpets.com/en/privacy/) separately.
