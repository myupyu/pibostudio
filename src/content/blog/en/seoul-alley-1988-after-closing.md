It’s 1988. You order tofu for tomorrow and set the prices for what’s on the shelf today. Customers come in after you open, and a receipt tells you how the day went after you close. Seeing what sold gives you a better idea of what to put out tomorrow.

## A day’s receipt and a month’s ledger

After a few days, sales alone tell you only so much about how the shop is doing. The ledger lists the cost of goods, spoilage, and operating expenses separately. Even when an item sells well, you’ll want to check whether you have enough cash to buy it again. At the end of the month, you’ll look at household expenses too.

## What will the shop look like next month?

The alley and the people who come through your door change over the years. When an opportunity to expand comes along, you can keep the shop as it is or change how it works in the same space. Open the monthly ledger to see the choices you made and how much room they left you for the following month.
