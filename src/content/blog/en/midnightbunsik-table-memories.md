A small object can remain in a seat after its guest has left. The shop in Midnight Bunsik is a place to grow, and also a place to remember meals you prepared together.

## Where would you like to sit?

The direct preparation path lets you choose a seat. Guests and dishes take their places within the shop you have decorated. The same favorite can belong to a different table.

## A little action after the words

The follow-up story also asks you to choose an action after a meal: wrapping something, covering a dish while you wait, or choosing a home for a memory. A different response and a keepsake follow what you decide to do.

We will leave the ending’s particular moments for you to discover. Look around your own shop to see the traces your choices have left.

## Time to revisit a memory

Read earlier stories again in recollections. Preview decorations before choosing how they belong in the shop. Stories you have finished do not need to be forced through again for progress.

## Still your shop when the door is closed

Once the main and follow-up stories conclude, service is optional. Reopen for a favorite dish, a guest’s response, or the table you have made. Or stay and rest.

Before a longer break, read [saving your shop and returning](/midnightbunsik/guides/save-and-return/).
