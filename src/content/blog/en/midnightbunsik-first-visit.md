On the first night, Mr. Walt comes in and asks for mild tteokbokki. As a reaper in training, you listen to his order and prepare a meal for him. Check the heat on the order slip before opening the cooking screen so you know how to season the dish.

## Make a bowl and stay a while

Add the rice cakes and fish cakes, then let the sauce simmer. After you serve the dish, you get to see the guest eat and hear what they think. The same guest can tell you something new or ask for a different level of spice the next time they come in. Their story comes out over several visits.

## What you can carry into the next order

If you fumble the first dish, you can try again. You can also serve what you made and keep following the guest’s story. The guest book gives you somewhere to revisit what they said and what they left behind.

The [first-meal guide](/midnightbunsik/guides/first-meal/) walks through the controls. See the shop and cooking screens on the [Midnight Bunsik page](/midnightbunsik/).
