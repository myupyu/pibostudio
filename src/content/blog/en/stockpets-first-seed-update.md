Plant your first seed, then look for a little sprout next to your pet when you get home. Turn a calendar page and your crops change in value while your pet grows a month older. In 1.0.2, it’s easier to see how that change carries through the farm, the room, and the album.

## See what you’ll take home before you harvest

Choose how much to harvest to see the coins you’ll receive and what will stay in the plot. You can harvest a portion and keep the rest growing until next month. Gifts and farm upgrades also show how many coins you’ll have left before you confirm.

## See when the next calendar page arrives

After the first 12 months, a calendar page refills every eight hours, up to three. When you run out of pages, the calendar shows when the next one is due. You can still care for your pet, tend the farm, and revisit earlier months in the album while you wait.

## Look at the whole album photo before sharing

Open a month in the album and tap Share preview to see the entire card before you share it. The card shows your pet, their room, and the in-game date.

Version 1.0.2 is available for iPhone on the Korean and US App Store. Read [how to play StockPets](/stockpets/guides/how-to-play/) to start your first farm. Android players can find StockPets on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.stockpets.app).
