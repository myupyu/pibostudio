Cubed radish kimchi is ripening in a floral jar. With market day coming up, you could scoop out a portion to sell. You could also leave it in the jar a little longer for a neighbor’s order. Either way, it’s the same batch, but the choice changes the next few days in the workshop.

## Take it to market

You need food ready to sell when market day comes. Open the jar and decide how much to bring, then think about what you want to make next. At the stall, check both the cost of packing a portion and the amount you’ll take home.

## Fill a neighbor’s order

The order board tells you what a neighbor needs, how much, and by when. If the kimchi is still young, open the jar to see when it might reach the next stage. The weather and where you keep the jar matter, too. When you take the order, you’ll have a date to work toward.
