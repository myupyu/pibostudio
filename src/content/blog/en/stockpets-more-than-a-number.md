A flourishing crop feels good. But in StockPets, something else might catch your eye first: the little companion waiting when you open the door.

## Who will share your first month?

Cat, dog, rabbit, hamster, squirrel, turtle, owl or bear: eight kinds of companions bring their own charm to the farm. Give your first pet a name and the little place starts to feel more like yours.

Choose a face you want to see again. Pixel ears, tiny tails and gentle movements add warmth to the journey. Whichever friend you pick, your first month in 1996 begins together.

## A small routine of food and play

On the farm, you choose seeds and consider when to harvest. At home, attention shifts to feeding, playing and cleaning. Basic care costs neither real money nor virtual coins.

After a profitable harvest, little extras such as treats can become part of the day. A result from the farm turns into time with your pet, giving those numbers a place in your little world.

## Growth is more than one number

Your companion grows from a baby to a young pet and then an adult as the months pass. Care and harvest experiences help shape its adult form. The question becomes not only how much you earned, but what time you spent together.

Even in a difficult month on the farm, your shared time keeps adding up. And a good harvest is no reason to forget care. Looking after both the crops and a companion gives StockPets a different rhythm from a game focused only on returns.

## Another generation, the same little farm

Once your pet is an adult, you can welcome a new generation. Your grown companion stays in the family garden, while the farm, coins and crops continue with the new pet. It is a continuing story rather than losing everything to start again.

Your first companion, your earliest seed, a memorable harvest, the month a new generation arrived: the longer you stay, the more layers the story gains. Years later, the friend beside you may be what you remember most.

## Time moves when you turn the calendar

Your journey advances when you choose the next month. The first 12 months are unrestricted. After that, one calendar charges every eight hours, up to three. You can advance one month or use three calendars together.

There is no need to rush. Spend a moment with your pet, check the farm and decide whether to turn the page. Choosing your own pace is part of the comfort of this little world.

## Keep your little story close

The game saves your journey on your device without a login or cloud sync. Before resetting or changing devices, export your record from Settings. It is a small habit to protect the story you have made.

You can start StockPets for free. Alongside wondering how your crops have changed, discover how your little companion has grown. A new friend is waiting on iPhone and Android.
